# Acceptable Use Policy

**Draft, awaiting professional legal review.**

You may use The Green Computer to analyse and improve AI models and hardware systems you are
entitled to work on.

You must not:

- upload files you have no right to share, or files that contain malware;
- try to read or change another workspace's projects, files or results, or probe the service for
  weaknesses without our written permission (report security problems to {{contact}});
- try to get around rate limits, file-size limits, authentication or authorisation;
- use the recommendation or export features to produce unlawful or harmful content, or try to
  make them ignore the platform's rules (for example by hiding instructions in uploaded
  documents);
- present predictions or directional what-ifs from the service as measured, simulated,
  certified or validated results to other people;
- use the service to design weapons or systems intended to cause harm;
- run automated sign-ups or scrape the service.

We may suspend accounts that break this policy.
