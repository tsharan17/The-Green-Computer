## 2. What the service does

The Green Computer predicts how an AI model would run on a hardware system: latency, memory,
power, energy, temperature, throttling, running cost and carbon. It recommends software and
hardware changes, lets you compare plans, imports measurements from real runs, and exports reports
and files. On this hosted service your models are **analysed, never executed**: timed runs happen
only on a device you pair yourself.
